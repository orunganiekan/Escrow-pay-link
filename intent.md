# Escrow Pay Links — Product Intent

## What It Is

**Escrow Pay Links** lets social-commerce sellers (on WhatsApp and Instagram) send Naira (₦) payment links where the buyer's money is held safely until delivery is confirmed.

## The Problem

- **Buyers** hesitate to transfer money upfront to sellers they found on social media because they have no protection if the item never arrives.
- **Sellers** cannot afford to ship goods on trust without knowing the buyer has already paid.

## Core Product Loop

1. **Create a Link:** A seller sets up a product (name, photo, price in ₦, and a delivery window such as 24h, 48h, 72h, or 7 days) and gets a short shareable link.
2. **Share Anywhere:** Pasting the link into WhatsApp or Instagram renders a rich preview card with the product photo, name, and price.
3. **Pay Without Friction:** A buyer opens the link on their phone, enters their delivery details (no account or password required), and pays in Naira.
4. **Ship With Confidence:** Once paid, funds are locked in escrow. The seller sees **"Payment secured, ship now"** and the buyer receives a **6-digit release code** plus a countdown to the delivery deadline.
5. **Resolve Cleanly:**
   - **Delivered:** On delivery, the buyer shares the 6-digit code with the seller. Entering the code releases the money to the seller's balance.
   - **Not Delivered:** If the delivery window expires without release, the buyer can claim a full refund.
   - **Disputed:** Before the deadline, the buyer can report a problem to freeze payout for review.

## Product Principles

- **Invisible Rails, Familiar Money:** Buyers and sellers only ever see Naira (₦), delivery windows, and a 6-digit release code—never crypto, wallets, or blockchain jargon. *(An optional, collapsed "Receipt on the chain" panel exists solely for verification/demos.)*
- **Zero-Signup Buying:** Buyers never have to create an account to pay, track their order, or claim a refund.
- **Earned Trust:** Product pages display the seller's track record (completion rate and completed order count) based on real fulfilled orders.
